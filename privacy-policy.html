Privacy Policy for Fermata Xtream

Effective date: May 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fermata Xtream ("the App") handles information. The App is a media player and related Android Auto companion functionality.

1. Information We Collect

The App is designed to minimize data collection. We do not sell personal data.

  • Media/library data: Local media metadata, playlists, and user settings stored on your device.
  • Network data: URLs/endpoints you provide (for example, streaming or playlist sources) to access your chosen content.
  • Voice/microphone input (optional): Used only when you use voice-related features that require microphone access.
  • Crash or diagnostic information: May be generated by Android/Google Play services for app stability and delivery.

2. Permissions and Why They Are Used

The App may request permissions such as:

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): For voice search/voice command features when enabled by you.
  • Storage/Media permissions: To read and play local media files and playlists.
  • Network permissions: To stream content from sources you configure.
  • Foreground service/media playback permissions: To support playback controls and Android Auto behavior.

You can deny permissions, but some features may not function properly.

3. How Data Is Used

  • To provide media playback and library features.
  • To execute features you explicitly use (for example, voice actions).
  • To improve reliability, performance, and compatibility.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data may be processed by third-party platform/service providers only as necessary to provide app functionality, distribution, or diagnostics (for example, Google Play services).

5. Data Retention

Most app data is stored locally on your device and remains until you delete it, clear app data, or uninstall the App.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is not specifically directed to children under 13. If you believe data from a child was provided inappropriately, contact us to request deletion where applicable.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no system is completely secure.

8. International Use

If you use the App outside your country, your data may be processed according to local platform and service infrastructure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective when the updated policy is published.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the developer via the Google Play Store listing.
